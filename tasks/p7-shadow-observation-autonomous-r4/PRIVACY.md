# No raw content enrichment

No raw user/model/candidate prose, target, raw arguments, clauses, source timestamp, result/P2 value or arbitrary exception/component_reason is read/retained. Complete approved canonical string arguments are used only for the frozen SHA256 preimage; no map is retained. Fixed errors avoid content-bearing diagnostics, including suppressed Unicode encoding details. Digests/IDs may remain linkable and do not anonymize data. The future private D04 retention/storage policy is separate.
