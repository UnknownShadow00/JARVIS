# Accounting and measurement validity

Distinguish A = eligible upstream evaluation attempts in an externally defined window; B = record submission calls; C = committed complete evidence entries; D = explicit nonaccepted submissions. The sink can observe a submitted call and its receipt, not an eligible turn never delivered to it. Submission means an actual sink call, not task scheduling or an in-memory queue insertion. Unknown post-crash completion is unresolved coverage, never assumed zero failure.

The strongest frozen local reconciliation requirement is **B = C** for accepted observation coverage, with D = 0, no unresolved commit/acknowledgment, integrity or duplicate ambiguity, and a recoverably complete submission ledger. These are necessary conditions, not sufficient proof of formal P7 acceptance. A nonaccepted COMMIT_UNCERTAIN receipt is not proof that no durable entry exists. An interrupted or acknowledgment-lost call may remain unknown until independently reconciled. Do not assume rejected/committed sets are disjoint over uncertain outcomes, subtract failure counts to invent coverage, or infer B from C.

A = B is NOT frozen: D03 records only eligible actual INITIAL_TURN terminal outcomes; context/envelope/binding/provider failures without a P7 terminal and producer failures may produce no record, and retries may yield multiple attempts per turn. Context V1 creates one turn per accepted input; it does not establish one evaluation attempt per turn. Identical record values do not identify attempts. No attempt ID, retry ordinal, client idempotency key or trace-to-turn equivalence is introduced.

Minimum upstream handoff for eventual measurement: authoritative scope start/end; eligible/admitted attempt accounting; recoverable submission accounting independent of successful writes; reconciliation of submissions/receipts/committed positions; nonterminal, producer, cancellation, submission, unknown-commit and duplicate coverage; and proof this accounting survived restarts. Same authoritative session/turn association is required where available; pre-context failure has no fabricated turn. D02/D07 must freeze attempt identity, concurrent/retry lifecycle and how pre-work intent becomes recoverable; D09/controller must freeze window boundaries. Aggregate counts alone cannot detect a missing entry compensated by a duplicate, so the associated set must also reconcile. No accounting-log module, schema, storage engine or scheduler is invented here.

Any known loss or failed submission makes the affected measurement interval/window **INCOMPLETE**; uncertainty/integrity corruption or unresolved duplicate accounting also prevents a complete claim. INCOMPLETE is an evidence-validity classification, not a new pipeline stop reason or operational event. A completed-record subset may remain readable but cannot erase the incomplete status. Only a future controller with complete authoritative reconciliation can establish coverage; persisted count, maximum sequence, timestamps or absent error logs cannot prove zero loss. No CT pass threshold/duration/sample count is selected.
