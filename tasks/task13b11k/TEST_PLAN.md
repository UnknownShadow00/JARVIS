## The scored matrix

fields compared per cell: `executor_called`, `result_status`, `error_kind`, `executed`,
`facts_source`, `confirmation_state_after`.

**10 of 150 cells reach an executor.** The other 140 refuse with `executed = false` and no facts.

(`ede846eb7ae4c295f2fbd76628e5b0509190513667856a0f6d50a1a68e71ce2e`) while
`app/execution/dispatch.py` did not exist; the test re-checks that digest before running.
