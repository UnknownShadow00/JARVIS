# Observational trust boundary

All source identity/control-plane facts come from their JARVIS owners. Model proposal count and guard status are observations about untrusted content, not permissions. The producer consumes settled outcomes, never derives control decisions. Its exact Python types and immutable output cannot alone prove origin authentication; trusted evaluator association is a precondition.

OperationalResponseSource and ResponseObligation are copied only as candidate labels. In inert initial-turn V1, TOOL_SUCCESS/TOOL_ERROR response sources and REPORT_TOOL_SUCCESS/REPORT_TOOL_ERROR obligations are out of scope and rejected rather than accepted as real-world evidence. Other candidate sources (including LEDGER or CONFIRMATION) do not assert new provenance truth or an approval claim. Conversational MODEL_RAW remains a source label without model text.

ALLOW is an observation of an actual P4 decision, not an executable grant held by this record. REQUIRE_CONFIRMATION means the existing policy requested a confirmation, never that one was claimed. proposal_match=True means complete consistency guard passage, never permission or execution. No external operational success, response replacement or trusted mutation can be represented by this schema. Future trusted execution evidence requires a separately authorized version/path.
