# Unavailable, timeout and missing completion

If the separately authorized provider cannot be called or returns no complete response, there is no authentic successful adapter output. Timeout, cancellation, broken stream or unavailable model cannot be replaced by zero proposals, empty ModelDraft, a fixture, deterministic projection or a different provider/model. No automatic fallback or hidden cache/replay success.

Do not call run_recorded_turn with an invented string solely to produce S05_ADAPTER/ADAPTER_INVALID for a provider failure: its recording field is required and there is no provider-unavailable/timeout enum in the passive API. Future evaluator/accounting must represent that pre-parser failure under a separately frozen settled diagnostic contract. Do not invent a new PipelineStopReason now. Legacy reply/stream/permission/confirmation/dispatch/external state remains unaffected.

The otherwise eligible attempt must be explicitly accounted for by D02/D07/controller. Do not silently drop it from measurement or choose final denominator/threshold here. A faithfully recorded provider failure is not automatically evidence loss; missing/unknown diagnostic/submission coverage prevents a complete claim under D04. Known evidence loss is INCOMPLETE; failed model behavior and lost evidence are distinct facts.

Partial stream chunks are not multiple complete canonical responses or zero-proposal outputs. D06 must define complete-response capture/normalization; D07 defines cancellation/retry selection and isolation. Existing legacy LLMClient recovery/audio/audit behavior is not authorized for shadow by this freeze.
