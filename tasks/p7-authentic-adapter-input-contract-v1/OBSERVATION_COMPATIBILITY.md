# Compatible upstream semantics; no schema amendment

D03 consumes already-settled facts and records parser proposal_count, actual proposal_match, current stage/reason/permission/candidate labels and authoritative turn/trace. D05 preserves each field's meaning: count=0 only after actual successful parsing; unavailable/invalid/unobserved remains None; match copied from actual full guard, not inferred from authentic origin. MODEL_RAW is a response-source label, never a live-origin category. Authenticity grants no permission or operational truth.

No existing D03 field can honestly carry provider/model identity, request/recording digest, live/replay/synthetic category or parser version. None was anticipated as an additive reserved field. Do not overload transport, trace, binding digest, candidate source or stop reason; no additive field or new observation semantics is frozen here.

Origin category and known invalid/unavailable/unobserved parser facts must be settled upstream before observation submission to the sink. Sink acceptance never triggers origin inference; later association of a durable receipt joins already-settled facts and cannot relabel replay/fixtures as live. D05 requires the upstream evaluator/controller to keep its associated authenticity evidence with D04's independently required scope/submission/evaluated-set reconciliation. Formal aggregation must join a stored entry to that evidence before model-proposal live eligibility. D04 receipt sequence/digest can identify the exact committed observation without becoming an attempt ID. This is a required future collector/controller contract, not a newly selected persistence path or a claim the current sink can store extra fields.

D04 consumes the exact 18-field record inside its six-field entry and remains persistence-only; it neither classifies authenticity nor selects measurement eligibility. A durable accepted sink receipt does not mean live-authentic, complete coverage, correct model or CT pass. Records from replay/synthetic tests remain observational and their respective denominators stay separate.

Therefore core D05 semantics require no change to frozen D03/D04, and compatibility is FROZEN with collection/runtime still blocked. If a later task instead requires self-contained authenticity fields inside either exact schema, that is an explicit incompatibility/versioned schema decision and must block that task until approved; no silent expansion authorized here. Missing separate authenticity evidence already blocks measured live scoring.
