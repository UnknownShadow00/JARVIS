# Offline security review — R2 candidate

The seven original findings have explicit corrections and regression coverage. This is engineering self-review, not the requested new independent Astra decision or human authorization. The canonical app, prior packets and prior evidence are unchanged. No host connection, NSS query, network/provider call, credential access, systemd/PVE invocation, callback install, VM/guest action or privileged qualification occurred.

Privilege separation remains conditional on complete fresh proof. Existing nobody/UID65534 is reused only if file-only identity/policy review independently establishes no VM-management authority, private credential access or supplementary privileged group and no unrelated activity. No account/ACL/sudo/PAM/NSS changes or authentication tests are permitted. The callback source remains byte-pinned and non-VM. The narrow future installer requires existing administrator custody; the service guard and every payload explicitly run UID/GID65534, with NoNewPrivileges and empty capability sets.

The recorder must construct a minimal LANG/LC_ALL/PATH parent environment before exec. Unit settings explicitly assign these values, clear PassEnvironment/EnvironmentFile/PAM/credentials and extra commands, disable login-environment setup and remove known loader/Python variables. A fresh nonsecret preactivation review must also establish safe manager/default environment, unit-type/name-prefix drop-ins, generators, loader/libraries, no dynamic overrides and exclusive administrator custody. `Environment=` or Python `-I` alone is not sufficient. No manager environment dump is a permitted inspection command. If safe nonsecret proof cannot be obtained, block. The guard's check is an additional barrier, not protection against a hostile dynamic loader that ran before it.

The proposed non-root ExecCondition validates actual UID/eUID/GID/eGID/groups, allowlisted environment keys and values, boot and both authorization bounds, with at least17 seconds remaining for start/runtime/stop budgets. It emits a public generation binding, not environment values. Failure exits255, preventing ExecStart under the documented ExecCondition model. Exact support, invocation-ID continuity and show serialization on systemd257 must still be established. A race after the guard, scheduler stalls, kernel failures, suspend or manager failure invalidate any claim of guaranteed expiration; none is made. R3 source/canary remain sealed.

Source review of bounded readers checks size before allocation, no-follow descriptor custody, postread identity, output expansion ceilings, finite resource/time caps and fail-closed malformed input. No exception logs supplied sensitive values. Gzip malformed CRC/trailer/deflate and aggregate expansion failures are rejected. Kernel-blocked I/O cannot be made physically time-bounded by Python; recorder timeout creates incomplete evidence, never PASS.

Strict raw readback syntax may reject actual systemd257 output. This is deliberately fail-closed, but every new reset/property/ExecCondition and compound ExecStart/TimersMonotonic representation needs exact-version documentation and eventual harmless readback. Existing local manuals retained by the audit are primary documentation for their installed versions only; they are not proof of pve's version. No unsupported fallback, sudo elevation, root service or parser weakening is authorized.

Residual risks, in priority order:

1. **HIGH / admission blocker:** missing authentic local-only policy, identity, credential-denial, manager/loader custody or recorder proof. No fixture can satisfy this.
2. **HIGH / operational limitation:** lost manager acknowledgement, uninterruptible I/O, expiry during failure or unreconciled ownership may leave pending work. Stop qualification; preserve evidence; only separately finite exact-owned recovery can follow expiry.
3. **MEDIUM / qualification uncertainty:** actual property support/serialization, cgroup layout/retention, WakeSystem/kernel support and exported clock domain. UNKNOWN fails. Physical wake, reboot/reexec survival and VM termination remain unqualified.
4. **MEDIUM / attainable-case uncertainty:** missingexec may be refused at creation; absent-dependency timer may never enter failed state; inactive transient service may be garbage-collected before strict final proof. Record incomplete, not a substitute PASS.
5. **MEDIUM / evidence trust:** hashes and schemas do not authenticate a human console or prove completeness of a fabricated journal. Independent externally held seals, append-only receipts and operator/reviewer custody remain prerequisites.

All failure evidence and partial artifacts are retained. There is no cleanup deletion, broad stop, automatic retry, same-name rearm, post-expiry continuation, account creation, package operation or VM route in the proposed command set.
