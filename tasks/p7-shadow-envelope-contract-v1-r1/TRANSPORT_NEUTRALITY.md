# Transport neutrality

The envelope has no framework/transport discriminator because downstream binding consumes only exact request, correlation and snapshot. Equal accepted texts with equivalent JARVIS contexts must produce equivalent authority-bearing fields across REST/WS. Fresh distinct turns legitimately differ in turn IDs and observations. Transport choice cannot change route, policy, permission, confirmation or provenance trust.

The composer remains reusable by a later voice adapter without claiming voice is implemented. It stops before adapter evaluation and remains scheduling-neutral.
