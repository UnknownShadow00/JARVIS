The signed payload keeps retirement EXPLICIT_OPERATOR_ZERO_USE_RETIREMENT separate from stop_attribution UNATTRIBUTED_V2_SHUTDOWN_HOOK. The old archive is never rewritten. An archive-only proof lacks the newly required fresh digests and cannot authorize reservation.

High-water schema is version 2 for a new isolated root. Existing roots are not migrated. Identity/deletion triggers protect ordinal, transport, session, commitment and permit, and the attempt join can transition from NULL once. Schema/inode/private-file checks and exact joins run before subsequent permits. Pending reservations burn capacity; crash and competing-reservation tests preserve that rule. Order remains REST, REST, WS, WS, with one scoped session per transport per the Gate 6B reviewed contract.

Coordinated backups now verify high-water schema, signed succession payload/root/bindings and permit commitments, even if a component's ordinary checksum is recomputed. Review restores remain exclusive, offline exports; they cannot reopen admissions or roll back an authority.

A same-UID actor can still replace/clone the complete coordinated authority and local state, or bypass Python APIs. The retained full-rollback test demonstrates this limit. A separately protected global root, approved identity design and disposable multi-UID qualification are mandatory before deployment. Local SQL triggers and hashes do not establish global antirollback, host-loss recovery or human presence.
